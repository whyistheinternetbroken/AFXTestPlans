== OPTIONAL: Snapshots and SnapMirror impact on performance

Snapshots and SnapMirror are integral to a complete data protection story for ONTAP. Part of their success is tied to their minimal impact on production workloads. The goal of this test is to confirm that snapshots and SnapMirror in operation during performance tests have little to no impact on the system performance.
